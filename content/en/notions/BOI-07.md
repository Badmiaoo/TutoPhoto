# BOI-07 · Aperture priority

## Explanation

In mode A, "Aperture-priority auto", you choose the aperture and the camera selects the shutter speed; the Nikon Z 6 manual suggests this mode to blur backgrounds or bring both foreground and background into focus ([Nikon, Z 7/Z 6 online manual, "The Mode Dial"](https://onlinemanual.nikonimglib.com/z7_z6/en/06_shooting_controls_01.html), read on 10 November 2026). When the shutter speed needed is beyond the camera's limits, the exposure indicator and the shutter speed flash: too much light, stop down; too little, open up (["Indicators and Error Messages"](https://onlinemanual.nikonimglib.com/z7_z6/en/13_troubleshooting_02.html)).

## Simulator

In the "Exposure" window, mode A. The "Shutter speed (auto)" dial is greyed out and shows the speed chosen; it flashes beyond the limits (30 s to 1/4000 s). With auto ISO, the sensitivity rises if the shutter speed would drop below the minimum.
