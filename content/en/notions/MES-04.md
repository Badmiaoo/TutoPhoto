# MES-04 · Exposure compensation

## Explanation

In modes other than auto, exposure compensation alters the exposure suggested by the camera to make the photo brighter or darker: from −5 to +5 EV in third-EV steps on the Nikon Z 6; a positive value brightens the subject, a negative value darkens it ([Nikon, Z 7/Z 6 online manual, "The E (Exposure Compensation) Button"](https://onlinemanual.nikonimglib.com/z7_z6/en/06_shooting_controls_03.html), read on 10 November 2026). In mode M, it only changes the exposure indicator, except with auto ISO, which takes it into account. It is not reset when the camera is turned off: remember to set it back to ±0 (same source).

## Simulator

In the "Exposure" window, opened by tapping the exposure indicator: from −5.0 to +5.0 EV in third steps. In A and S, the camera changes the shutter speed or the aperture and the indicator shows the compensation; in M, only the indicator moves, except with auto ISO. Compensation is remembered, as on the Z 6.

## Exercise

The reference sheet suggests making snow white; the park scene has none, so the exercise is adapted. In mode A at f/8, shoot without compensation, then with +1 EV: the report goes from about −0.2 EV to +0.8 EV (the camera lengthens the exposure from 1/100 s to 1/50 s). Do it again in M without auto ISO: the photo does not change, only the indicator moves.
