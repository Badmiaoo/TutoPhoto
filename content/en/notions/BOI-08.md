# BOI-08 · Shutter priority

## Explanation

In mode S, "Shutter-priority auto", you choose the shutter speed and the camera selects the aperture; the Nikon Z 6 manual suggests this mode to freeze or blur motion ([Nikon, Z 7/Z 6 online manual, "The Mode Dial"](https://onlinemanual.nikonimglib.com/z7_z6/en/06_shooting_controls_01.html), read on 10 November 2026). When the aperture needed is beyond the lens's limits, the exposure indicator and the aperture flash: too little light, use a slower shutter speed or a higher ISO; too much, a faster shutter speed (["Indicators and Error Messages"](https://onlinemanual.nikonimglib.com/z7_z6/en/13_troubleshooting_02.html)).

## Simulator

In the "Exposure" window, mode S. The "Aperture (auto)" dial is greyed out and shows the aperture chosen; it flashes beyond the limits of the virtual lens (f/1.4 to f/22). With auto ISO, the sensitivity changes when the aperture is not enough.

## Exercise

The reference sheet asks to freeze a child running at the end of the day; in the park, it is the dog. In mode S at 1/1000 s, AF-C and auto-area AF with animal detection, pass challenge SIT-06. Then go to 1/4000 s without auto ISO: the aperture flashes at f/1.4 and the photo is underexposed; turn on auto ISO and try again.
