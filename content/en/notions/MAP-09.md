# MAP-09 · Eye, face, subject detection

## Explanation

In auto-area AF, the Nikon Z 6 detects the subject and selects the area; with face and eye detection, it focuses on one or the other of the subject's eyes, or on the face if the eyes are not detected ([Nikon, Z 7/Z 6 online manual, "Focus"](https://onlinemanual.nikonimglib.com/z7_z6/en/05_basic_settings_01.html), read on 9 November 2026). Setting a4 chooses what is detected: faces and eyes, faces only, dogs and cats, or nothing; people and animals are not detected at the same time ([setting a4](https://onlinemanual.nikonimglib.com/z7_z6/en/09_menu_guide_05_a04.html)). Tapping a face makes the camera track it; detection may fail if the face takes up a very large or very small part of the frame, or if the eyes are hidden ("Focus").

## Simulator

"Focus" window, auto-area AF and "Auto-area AF face/eye detection". The marker surrounds the eye or face chosen, in amber, then in green once locked in AF-S. An eye is seen if it is in the covered area and nothing hides it. The manual gives no size thresholds: TutoPhoto detects a face when the spacing of the eyes is 0.4% to 25% of the frame's long side, and the eyes from 0.8%. Light, glasses and hair do not affect detection.
