# BOI-12 · Drive modes

## Explanation

The release mode of the Nikon Z 6 ([online manual, "The Release Mode/Self-Timer Button"](https://onlinemanual.nikonimglib.com/z7_z6/en/06_shooting_controls_04.html), read on 10 November 2026):
- **Single frame**: one photo each time the shutter-release button is pressed;
- **Continuous L**: 1 to 5 frames per second while the button is held down (3 by default);
- **Continuous H**: up to about 5.5 frames per second; **Continuous H (extended)**: 12 frames per second on the Z 6;
- **Self-timer**: 2, 5, 10 or 20 s (10 by default, ["Defaults"](https://onlinemanual.nikonimglib.com/z7_z6/en/09_menu_guide_01.html)).

## Simulator

Release mode button. In burst mode, one frame per interval of 1 / frame rate, never shorter than the exposure; each frame is computed at its own instant, with focus measured again in AF-C. The review screen scrolls through the series, with a report for each frame. TutoPhoto's limit: 20 frames per burst. Not simulated: the memory buffer, silent mode.

## Exercise

The reference sheet suggests catching the top of a jump; the dog in the scene does not jump, so the exercise is adapted. In Continuous H, with challenge SIT-06, shoot a burst as the dog passes and find in the series the frame where it is best placed in the frame and passes the challenge.
