# MAP-05 · AF area modes

## Explanation

The AF-area mode says which part of the frame is used to focus. On the Nikon Z 6 ([online manual, "Focus"](https://onlinemanual.nikonimglib.com/z7_z6/en/05_basic_settings_01.html), read on 9 November 2026):
- **Single-point AF**: the camera focuses on a point selected by the user; it is the default setting;
- **Wide-area AF (S) and (L)**: like single-point AF, over a wider area, the (L) areas being larger than the (S) areas; if the area contains subjects at different distances, the camera gives priority to the closest one;
- **Auto-area AF**: the camera detects the subject and selects the area itself (see MAP-09).

## Simulator

"Focus" window: single-point, wide-area (S), wide-area (L), auto-area. The viewfinder marker shows the area measured. The manual does not give the size of the wide areas: TutoPhoto chose squares of 10% (S) and 20% (L) of the frame's long side. The Z 6's dynamic-area and pinpoint AF are not simulated.
