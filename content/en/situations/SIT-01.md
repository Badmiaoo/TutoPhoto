# SIT-01 · Portrait in open shade, overcast

## Where, when, what, why

Outdoors, during the day: the park under an overcast sky. The subject is Sophia, in the middle of the group, about 6.6 m away. The intention: isolate the subject, with sharp eyes.

## Recipe

The situation solver will come in V2; this recipe is written by hand, with TutoPhoto's camera (Nikon Z 6, 50 mm).
- **Mode A, f/2**: you choose the aperture for depth of field (BOI-07). At 50 mm, focused at 6.6 m, the sharp zone goes from 5.7 m to 7.8 m: the bin (10.5 m), the dog (9 m) and the walker (12.5 m), behind her, fall outside it (NET-01).
- **AF-S, auto-area AF, face and eye detection**: the camera focuses on the eye (MAP-04, MAP-09). In single-point AF, you have to aim at the eye yourself (MAP-06).
- **Auto ISO**: at f/2 and ISO 100, the camera takes about 1/1600 s.
- **Plan B**: without opening up further, get closer to the subject (NET-06) or use a longer focal length (NET-01).

## Challenge

Challenge SIT-01, in the "Challenges" window: Sophia's eye in the frame and in the sharp zone, and the background behind her outside the sharp zone.

## Notions

MAP-06 AF points; MAP-09 eye, face, subject detection; NET-01 depth of field; NET-06 subject-background distance; LUM-02 hard, soft light.
