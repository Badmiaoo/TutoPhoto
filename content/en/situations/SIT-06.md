# SIT-06 · Running child or dog

## Where, when, what, why

A park, at the end of the day: the dog runs side-on, about 9 m away, at 3.5 m/s. The intention: freeze a sharp subject. TutoPhoto's scene has fixed light, that of an overcast sky; the evening drop in light is not simulated.

## Recipe

Recipe written by hand (the solver will come in V2), TutoPhoto's camera (Nikon Z 6, 50 mm).
- **Mode S, 1/1000 s**: you choose the shutter speed to freeze (BOI-08). The dog then leaves 0.019 mm on the sensor, 0.8 px on the photo, under the acceptable blur; at least 1/800 s is needed (MVT-01).
- **Auto ISO**: if the aperture is no longer enough, the sensitivity rises (MES-10). Here, at ISO 100, the camera takes about f/2.5, and the sharp zone around 9 m goes from 7.2 m to 12.1 m.
- **AF-C, auto-area AF, animal detection**: focus follows the dog (MAP-04, MAP-05, MAP-09).
- **Continuous H**: a burst as it passes, then sort the series (BOI-12).

## Challenge

Challenge SIT-06: the dog in the frame, in the sharp zone, and frozen.

## Notions

MAP-04 single AF, continuous AF; MAP-05 AF area modes; MVT-01 freezing motion; MES-10 auto ISO; BOI-12 drive modes; BOI-08 shutter priority.
