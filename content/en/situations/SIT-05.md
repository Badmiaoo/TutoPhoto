# SIT-05 · Group photo

## Where, when, what, why

Indoors or outdoors; here, the park. The group: Dennis, Sophia, Fabienne and Percy, side by side about 6.5 m away. The intention: everyone sharp.

## Recipe

Recipe written by hand (the solver will come in V2), TutoPhoto's camera (Nikon Z 6, 50 mm).
- **Mode A, f/8**: at 50 mm, focused at 6.5 m, the sharp zone goes from 4.1 m to 16 m (NET-01). The group stands in a single row: a small aperture is not essential, but it leaves a margin if focus falls slightly in front or behind.
- **Auto-area AF, face and eye detection**, or single-point AF on a face in the middle (MAP-05, MAP-09). Beware of wide-area AF: it takes the closest subject, often the ground in front of the feet (MAP-05).
- **Auto ISO**: at f/8 and ISO 100, the camera takes about 1/100 s; the group is still.

## Challenge

Challenge SIT-05: the eyes of Dennis, Sophia, Fabienne and Percy in the frame and in the sharp zone.

## Notions

NET-01 depth of field; MAP-05 AF area modes; MAP-09 eye, face, subject detection.
